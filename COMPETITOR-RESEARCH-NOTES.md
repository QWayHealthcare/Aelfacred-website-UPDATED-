## Editorial interpretation

The common buying themes are organized provider data, recurring credential work,
clear responsibilities, enrollment follow-up, operational reporting, and the mix of
software and professional services. The additions turn those themes into useful
questions and a concrete account of the handoff between teams.

The new “Why QWay developed AelfaCred” section is purpose/positioning copy grounded
in the existing product description and QWay's published service context. No founding
date, founder quotation, development event, customer interview or numerical result
has been invented. The narrative explains the operational problem and intended value;
it is not presented as a documented chronology of product development.

## Workbook limitations retained in the research record

- The comparison column is labeled QCRED; its exact relationship to the current
  AelfaCred release is not established by the workbook.
- Auto-fill claims conflict with parts of the existing site. Existing copy was retained
  as requested; new copy does not add an auto-fill availability promise.
- The 5.5K+ form count, named integrations, automated PSV scope, claims tracking,
  accreditations and competitor capability restrictions were not adopted as facts.
- The historical chart and CVO explainer are reference material, not independent
  verification of current product editions or accreditation scope.
- SourceForge's sponsored directory ordering and vendor descriptions are not a
  substantiated ranking. No claim that AelfaCred outperforms a named vendor is made.

## What was added

Home: a contextual-status section. About: the QWay/AelfaCred purpose narrative.
Platform: an interactive demonstration explorer. Services: an enrollment-to-billing
handoff section. Resources: a selectable demo-planning checklist. A new
`/why-aelfacred/` page explains the approach and provides an evaluation framework.
