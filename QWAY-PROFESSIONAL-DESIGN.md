# QWay professional brand treatment

The About-page QWay visual now uses a crisp ivory oval, a fine blue perimeter
accent and the original, unfiltered red-and-blue wordmark. Supporting text sits
within the curved composition; three numbered service labels are arranged below
with fine dividing rules. A short blue rule expands subtly on desktop hover.

This replaces the previous animated sculpture. There is no canvas or continuous
animation in this visual. Its layout works without JavaScript and its hover
transition respects reduced-motion preferences.

Existing surrounding copy and previous website updates are retained. This note
supersedes the earlier QWay highlight/sculpture design notes.
