WHY AELFACRED

Why AelfaCred:
Know Where Every Credentialing File Stands

AelfaCred is your one-stop solution for simplifying and managing the entire credentialing process. One dashboard shows which stage each file has reached, what is still missing, and who is following up.

Nobody has to dig through separate records and messages to find out.

Book a Demo↗
01

Software and Specialists in One Workflow

Use AelfaCred to run credentialing in-house, add specialist support, or split the tasks between your team and ours. When our specialists are involved, you follow progress in the same dashboard instead of waiting on status reports.

02

Less Retyping

Supported application forms fill in from the provider information you have stored. Your team reviews the details and completes whatever is missing before submission.

03

Expiration Dates and Deadlines Next to Each File

License expirations, CAQH attestation dates, document expirations, and renewal deadlines sit on the provider record. Automatic alerts show what is coming up, so updated documents can be requested early.

04

Clear Responsibilities

You know which tasks your team owns and which ones our specialists handle. We agree on the scope before work starts and revisit it when your roster or workload changes.

05

Built by a Billing Company

AelfaCred comes from QWay Healthcare, a medical billing and revenue cycle management company. We know billing waits on enrollment, so the platform keeps enrollment status, confirmed effective dates, and open requirements beside the credentialing tasks behind them.
